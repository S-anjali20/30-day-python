# ⌨️ Day 14 — Typing Speed Test

A fun and interactive typing speed application built with Python and Pygame as part of my **30 Days, 30 Python Projects Challenge**!

Test your typing speed, improve your accuracy, and challenge yourself with randomly generated words! 🚀

---

## 🎮 About the Project

**Typing Speed Test** is an interactive desktop application where users type randomly generated word sequences within a limited time. Choose between 30-second and 60-second challenges, track your Words Per Minute (WPM), and monitor your accuracy in real time.

Correct characters are highlighted in green, while incorrect characters appear in red. The application also tracks mistakes and saves your personal-best WPM and accuracy locally, making it easier to monitor your progress over time.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎲 **Random Word Generation:** Generate a fresh sequence of words for every new test.
- ⏱️ **Multiple Time Modes:** Choose between 30-second and 60-second typing challenges.
- 🚀 **Live WPM Tracking:** Calculate and display typing speed in real time.
- 🎯 **Accuracy Tracking:** Monitor typing accuracy as you type.
- 🟢 **Character Highlighting:** Highlight correct characters in green and incorrect characters in red.
- ❌ **Mistake Counter:** Track incorrect characters and total characters typed.
- 🏆 **Personal Best Tracking:** Save and load your highest WPM and best accuracy locally.
- 📊 **Results Dashboard:** View final WPM, accuracy, correct characters, and mistakes after each test.
- 🔄 **Quick Restart:** Start another challenge or return to the home screen.
- 🎨 **Modern Dark UI:** Enjoy a clean interface with rounded cards and colorful buttons.
- ⌨️ **Keyboard Controls:** Use Backspace to correct typing and Esc to return home or exit.
